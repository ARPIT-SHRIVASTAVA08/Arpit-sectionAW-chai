Method

  1. Boil the water with ginger and cardamom for two minutes.
  2. Add the tea leaves and simmer for the time shown above.
  3. Pour in the milk and bring it to a rise. Lower the heat before it overflows.
  4. Repeat the rise twice more for a deeper colour.
  5. Stir in sugar, strain, and serve hot.

Questions

Why add the milk late?

Tea boiled in milk from the start stays pale and flat. Brewing in water first pulls out the flavour.

Can I use tea bags?

Yes. Use one bag per cup and cut the simmer time by a minute.

What if I don't have cardamom?

Skip it, or use a small pinch of fennel seeds. Ginger alone still makes a good cup.
